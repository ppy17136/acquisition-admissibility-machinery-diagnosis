# Data provenance and redistribution boundary

Source: Paderborn University Bearing DataCenter
https://mb.uni-paderborn.de/kat/forschung/bearing-datacenter

Frozen bearings: K001-K005, KA04, KA15, KA16, KA22, KA30, KI04, KI14, KI16, KI18 and KI21. The release does not contain the downloaded RAR or extracted MAT files. `primary/provenance/SOURCE_ARCHIVE_CHECKSUMS.csv` records official URLs, archive sizes, SHA-256 digests and successful integrity checks. The non-raw log-PSD cache and derived feature/evidence tables are supplied for non-commercial scientific verification with source attribution and remain subject to the source dataset's terms.
