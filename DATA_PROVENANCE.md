# Data provenance, licence and redistribution boundary

Source: Paderborn University Bearing DataCenter
https://mb.uni-paderborn.de/kat/forschung/bearing-datacenter

The source dataset is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-
NonCommercial 4.0 International licence (CC BY-NC 4.0). Non-commercial
academic use requires attribution to the Paderborn University Bearing
DataCenter and its cited benchmark publication.

Frozen bearings: K001-K005, KA04, KA15, KA16, KA22, KA30, KI04, KI14,
KI16, KI18 and KI21. The release does not contain the downloaded RAR or
extracted MAT files. `primary/provenance/SOURCE_ARCHIVE_CHECKSUMS.csv`
records official URLs, archive sizes, SHA-256 digests and successful
integrity checks. The non-raw log-PSD cache and derived feature, prediction
and evidence tables are supplied for non-commercial scientific verification
with source attribution and remain subject to CC BY-NC 4.0 and the source
dataset's terms. See `LICENSES.md` for the file-level licence map.
